Crazy Game is free. Its challenges are hostile on purpose: controls that lie, obstacles that fight back, answers you have to work out. That is the game, not a fault.

A nickname has 2 to 20 characters and must be unique, ignoring capital letters. Linked players' nicknames appear on the public scoreboard. A nickname must not contain:

  • Advertising, links, or anything selling something.

How nicknames are moderated

  • A word filter refuses names containing offensive words when you choose them.
  • Any player can report a name from the scoreboard. When three players who have linked Google report the same name, it is hidden from every board until it is reviewed.
  • The developer reviews reported names. A name that breaks these terms is removed: nobody can choose that exact name again, and its owner is asked to choose another. Their solves and points are kept.
  • A reported name that does not break these terms is shown again.
