CRAZYGAME

Delete your account

Crazy Game is a puzzle game for Android by bonaxl015. This page explains how to delete your account and the data that goes with it.

In the game

  1. Open Profile from the home screen.
  2. Tap Delete account, under Send diagnostics.
  3. Tap Delete for good.

Your account and its data are deleted straight away. The game then starts again as a new player.

Without the game

Email bonaxl015@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Crazy Game account”.

  • If you linked a Google account, send the email from that Google address. That is how we know the account is yours.
  • If you never linked Google, tell us your nickname.

We handle requests within 7 days and reply once your account is deleted.

Email a deletion request

What is deleted

Your nickname, points, solved challenges, challenge attempts, clues used, the nickname reports you made or received, the link to your Google account, and your sign-in sessions and security logs.

What is kept

  • If a moderator removed your nickname, that name stays on a list of blocked names. The list does not record who used it.
  • Backups of the game's database are deleted within 30 days, and your data with them.

Your Google account

Deleting your Crazy Game account does not touch your Google account. To also remove Crazy Game from your Google account, open Third-party apps & services.
