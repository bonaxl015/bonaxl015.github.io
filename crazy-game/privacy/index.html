CRAZYGAME

Privacy policy

Crazy Game is a puzzle game for Android by bonaxl015. This policy explains what the game collects, why, and how to have it deleted. Questions go to bonaxl015@gmail.com.

What the game stores

Every player starts anonymous. The first time you open the game it creates an account with a random ID and no name, email address or password. The game then stores:

  • The nickname you choose, and when a moderator removed one, if that ever happens.
  • For each challenge you open: the random seed that sets its obstacles, how long you have played it, how many answers you submitted, and when and how fast you solved it.
  • Your score for each solved challenge, and when you used a daily clue.
  • Nickname reports you made or received, with the name reported.
  • If you link a Google account: its email address and the basic profile Google shares with apps you sign in to, which is your name and a link to your profile picture. The game shows only the email, on your Profile.
  • Sign-in sessions and security logs, which record the IP address and the type of device each sign-in came from.

The game uses this to run your account, keep your progress, rank linked players on the scoreboard, enforce the nickname rules, and limit how fast answers can be submitted.

What the game never collects

  • Your answers. Only the number of answers you submitted is kept.
  • Sound, movement, brightness or battery. Some challenges read the microphone level, the motion sensors, the screen brightness or the battery level on your phone. Those readings stay on the phone: nothing is recorded, kept or sent.
  • Advertising IDs, analytics or tracking of any kind. The game shows no ads.

What stays on your phone

Your sign-in session, in the phone's secure storage; a copy of your player, so the game opens quickly; the challenge list; and an error log. The error log never leaves your phone unless you tap Send diagnostics on your Profile and choose where to send it.

Who else is involved

  • Supabase hosts the game's servers and database, where everything above is stored.
  • Google provides Google sign-in, if you use it, and distributes the game through Google Play.
  • Expo delivers updates to the game. When the game checks for one, Expo receives the request, including your IP address, the game's version and a random ID that identifies this installation of the game.
  • Unsplash serves the photos in one challenge. Your phone requests them from Unsplash directly.

The game sells nothing about you and shares nothing for advertising.

What is public

If you link Google, your nickname and points appear on the scoreboard, which every player can see. Players who have not linked Google are not ranked. Your email address is never shown to other players.

How long it is kept

Until you delete your account. Backups of the game's database are deleted within 30 days, and your data with them.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account and its data in the game, or ask by email.

How to delete your account

Children

Crazy Game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email bonaxl015@gmail.com and it will be deleted.
